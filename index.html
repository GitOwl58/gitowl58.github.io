The Obsidian Vault

Every course module, every book, every night spent breaking a lab VM ends up here first. It's a personal knowledge base built in Obsidian, structured as a dense graph rather than a pile of folders. MOCs (Maps of Content) sit at the center of each domain, linking out to concept notes, tool references, and journal entries.

The rule I hold myself to: concept notes explain, tool notes just point. A note on IoT security carries a real write-up; a note on Nmap just links to the lessons and MOCs that use it. Everything gets a #domain: tag so the graph view actually means something instead of turning into noise.

I use my free time to journal what I learn, the lessons I follow, and update my vault daily.
